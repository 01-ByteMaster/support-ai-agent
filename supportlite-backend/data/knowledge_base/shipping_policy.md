

# Shipping Policy — DEMONSTRATION DATA

Document ID: shipping_policy
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-01-01
Applies to: All physical orders delivered within India
Related documents: cancellation_policy, order_modification_policy, support_procedures

## Shipping Summary
Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days and delivered in 3 to 7 business days with standard shipping. Express shipping delivers in 1 to 3 business days where available. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

## Serviceable Areas
SupportLite Store delivers to most pin codes in India. Serviceability is checked at checkout by entering the pin code. International shipping is not available. Some remote locations may have longer delivery times or may not be serviceable.

## Order Processing Time
- Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment confirmation.
- Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, on weekends, or on holidays start processing on the next business day.
- Made-to-order or personalized items may need an additional 3 to 5 business days, shown on the product page.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times
| Method | Estimated delivery time after dispatch | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 7 business days | All serviceable pin codes |
| Express | 1 to 3 business days | Selected metro and major city pin codes |

Delivery estimates are calculated from dispatch, not from the order date.

## Shipping Charges
- Standard shipping is free for orders of ₹999 or more after discounts.
- Standard shipping for orders below ₹999 is ₹60.
- Express shipping is charged at ₹150, regardless of order value.
- Cash on Delivery orders may carry an additional handling fee of ₹40, shown at checkout.
Final shipping charges are always shown at checkout before payment.

## Order Tracking
Once an order is dispatched, the customer receives an email and SMS with the courier name and tracking number. Tracking is also available in My Orders. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Order Status Meaning
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Placed | Order received, payment pending or being verified |
| Confirmed | Payment verified, order being prepared |
| Packed | Items packed and awaiting courier pickup |
| Shipped | Handed to the courier and in transit |
| Out for Delivery | Courier is delivering the parcel today |
| Delivered | Parcel delivered to the address or receiver |

## Delivery Attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail because the customer is unavailable, the address is incorrect, or the phone is unreachable, the parcel is returned to the warehouse. Refund or reshipment after a failed delivery follows the cancellation_policy; the shipping fee may be deducted or a reshipment fee charged.

## Delayed Orders
If an order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date:
1. Check the tracking page for the latest status.
2. Wait one additional business day, as couriers sometimes update status late.
3. Contact support with the order ID if the order is more than 2 business days late. Support will contact the courier for an update.
Delays caused by weather, strikes, natural disasters, regional restrictions, or public holidays are outside SupportLite Store's control, and compensation is not guaranteed for such delays.

## Lost or Missing Packages
If tracking shows Delivered but the customer did not receive the parcel:
1. Check with neighbours, family members, building security, or reception.
2. Report it to support within 48 hours of the Delivered status.
3. Support raises a trace with the courier, which can take up to 7 business days.
If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, the customer can choose a free reshipment (subject to stock) or a full refund.
If the tracking shows no movement for 10 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, the order is treated as potentially lost and investigated.

## Damaged Packages
If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, the customer should photograph the package before and after opening it, and report the issue within 48 hours of delivery. Where possible, customers should note damage on the courier's delivery receipt. See refund_policy for damaged items.

## Incorrect or Incomplete Addresses
Customers are responsible for providing a complete and correct address and an active phone number. Orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer are not eligible for free reshipment. Address changes before dispatch are covered by the order_modification_policy.

## Delivery to Others and Contactless Delivery
Parcels may be handed to a family member, security, or reception at the address. Customers who prefer to receive the parcel personally can add delivery instructions at checkout, but these are not guaranteed by the courier. Some high-value orders may require an OTP at delivery.

## Split Shipments
Orders with multiple items may be shipped in separate parcels, from different warehouses, and arrive on different dates. Shipping is not charged twice for split shipments of a single order.
