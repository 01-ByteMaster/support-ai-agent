## Orders and Tracking

### How do I track my order?
Open My Orders on the website or app and select the order to see its status and tracking link. After dispatch you also receive the courier name and tracking number by email and SMS. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

### What do the order statuses mean?
Placed means the order is received. Confirmed means payment is verified. Packed means it is ready for pickup. Shipped means it is with the courier. Out for Delivery means it will arrive today. Delivered means it has been handed over.

### I did not receive an order confirmation. What should I do?
Check your spam folder and My Orders. If the order does not appear and money was deducted, contact support with the payment reference. Failed payments are normally reversed automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

## Shipping and Delivery

### How long does delivery take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch, and express shipping takes 1 to 3 business days where available. Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

### How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free for orders of ₹999 or more and ₹60 below that. Express shipping is ₹150. Cash on Delivery may include a ₹40 handling fee. The exact cost appears at checkout.

### Do you deliver internationally?
No. SupportLite Store currently delivers only within India.

### My order is late. What should I do?
Check the tracking page first. If the order is more than 2 business days past the estimated date, contact support with your order ID.

### Tracking says delivered but I did not get my order.
Check with neighbours, family, and building security, then report it to support within 48 hours of the Delivered status. Support will trace the parcel with the courier.

## Cancellations

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, if it has not been dispatched. Orders in Placed, Confirmed, or Packed status can be cancelled free of charge. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery or return it after delivery.

### How do I cancel an order?
Go to My Orders, select the order, and choose Cancel Order. Or contact support with the order ID. You receive a confirmation by email and SMS.

### How long after cancellation will I get my money back?
For prepaid orders, the refund is initiated within 3 business days. The time for it to show in your account depends on your bank or payment provider and no fixed date is promised. Typical settlement is 5 to 10 business days.

## Returns and Refunds

### What is the return window?
You can return eligible items within 7 calendar days of delivery.

### What is the refund policy?
Refund requests may be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible, unused products in original packaging. Final-sale items are not eligible. Approved refunds go to the original payment method, and bank processing time varies.

### Which items cannot be returned?
Final-sale items, gift cards, digital products, personalized items, and hygiene-sensitive products with broken seals cannot be returned unless defective or incorrect.

### I received a damaged or wrong item. What do I do?
Report it within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and clear photos or a video. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund, and return shipping is free.

### Do I have to pay for return shipping?
Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or wrong items. For change-of-mind or size returns a ₹80 return shipping fee may be deducted from the refund.

### How do I return a product?
Open My Orders, choose Return Item, select the reason, receive an RMA number, pack the item with its original packaging, and hand it to the courier at pickup. See the return policy for details.

### When will I get my refund?
After the returned item is inspected (within 2 business days of arrival), approved refunds are initiated within 3 business days. Your bank then decides how quickly the money shows, usually 5 to 10 business days.

### My refund has not arrived. What should I do?
Check that the refund status shows Initiated or Refunded. Wait up to 10 business days after initiation. If it still has not arrived, contact support with the order ID and the refund reference number.

### I paid by Cash on Delivery. How do I get a refund?
COD refunds are paid by bank transfer or UPI to an account you provide, or as store credit. They are not returned in cash.

### Can I get store credit instead of a refund?
Yes. Store credit is issued immediately after approval, is valid for 12 months, and cannot be converted to cash.

### Can I exchange an item?
Yes, within 7 calendar days of delivery for size, colour, or variant, subject to stock. If the variant is not available you can choose a refund.

## Order Changes

### Can I change my delivery address?
Yes, once, before the order is dispatched and if the new pin code is serviceable. After dispatch the address cannot be changed.

### Can I change the item, size, or quantity?
Not directly. Cancel the order before dispatch and place a new order with the correct items.

### Can I add an item to an existing order?
No. Place a new order. Shipping is charged separately.

### Can I change the payment method after ordering?
No. Cancel the order before dispatch and place a new one with the preferred payment method.

## Warranty and Product Support

### Do products have a warranty?
Eligible products have a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date. Accessories have a 6-month warranty.

### What does the warranty not cover?
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, normal wear and tear, consumables, and misuse are not covered.

### How do I make a warranty claim?
Contact support with your order ID, serial number if applicable, and photos or a video of the defect. If approved you receive an RMA number and a free pickup or shipping instructions.

### How long does a warranty repair take?
Inspection takes 3 to 5 business days after the product reaches the service center. Repairs typically take 7 to 14 business days.

## Payments and Account

### What payment methods are accepted?
Debit and credit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, and Cash on Delivery where available. Available methods are shown at checkout.

### I was charged twice. What should I do?
Contact support immediately with the payment reference numbers. Duplicate charges are treated as urgent and the extra amount is refunded after verification.

### Is it safe to share my card details with support?
No. Support will never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, password, or OTP. Do not share them with anyone.

## Contacting Support

### How can I contact support?
Chat is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Email is support@supportlite.example with replies in 24 business hours. Phone support is Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST.

### Can I speak to a human agent?
Yes. Ask for a human agent at any time and your case will be passed to the support team with the details you have already provided.
