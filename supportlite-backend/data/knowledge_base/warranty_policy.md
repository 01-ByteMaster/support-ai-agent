
# Warranty Policy — DEMONSTRATION DATA

## Warranty Summary
Eligible products are covered by a limited warranty against manufacturing defects for 12 months from the delivery date. Accessories and bundled items are covered for 6 months. The warranty covers repair, replacement, or refund, but does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, or unauthorized repair.

## Warranty Coverage Period
- Main products: 12 months from the date of delivery.
- Accessories (chargers, cables, cases, remotes): 6 months from the date of delivery.
- Replacement products: covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or 3 months, whichever is longer.
The warranty period shown on the product page or invoice applies if it differs from the above.

## What the Warranty Covers
The limited warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship that occur during normal use, such as:
- Product fails to power on or stops working without external damage.
- Components malfunction when used as intended.
- Manufacturing faults visible within the warranty period.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage such as drops, cracks, dents, or bending.
- Liquid damage, water damage, or exposure to moisture beyond the product's stated rating.
- Damage from power surges, wrong voltage, or non-original chargers.
- Normal wear and tear, scratches, fading, and cosmetic damage.
- Consumables such as batteries (after 6 months), filters, ear tips, and straps.
- Products repaired, opened, or modified by anyone other than authorized service centers.
- Loss, theft, or damage during shipping arranged by the customer.
- Products with removed, altered, or unreadable serial numbers.
- Software issues caused by third-party apps, unofficial updates, or user modification.

## Warranty vs Return Policy
The return policy applies for 7 calendar days after delivery and allows a refund for eligible unused products. The warranty applies for a longer period but covers only defects, not change of mind. If a defect appears within the first 7 days, the customer may choose a return or a warranty claim.

## Documents Required for a Warranty Claim
- Order ID or invoice number.
- Registered name, email address, or phone number.
- Product serial number or IMEI, if applicable.
- Description of the problem and when it started.
- Photos or a short video showing the defect.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim
1. Contact support with the order ID and the documents listed above.
2. Support reviews the claim and may request troubleshooting steps, for example a reset or a different charger.
3. If the problem is not resolved, the claim is approved for service and an RMA number is issued.
4. The customer sends the product to the service center, or free pickup is arranged where available.
5. The product is inspected within 3 to 5 business days of arrival at the service center.

## Warranty Claim Outcomes
After inspection, SupportLite Store will choose one of the following, depending on the defect and stock:
- Repair the product at no cost.
- Replace it with an identical or equivalent product.
- Refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not possible.
Repairs typically take 7 to 14 business days from the product's arrival at the service center. Timelines may be longer for parts that must be imported.

## Warranty Shipping Costs
- Pickup and return shipping are free for approved warranty claims.
- If a claim is found not eligible, the customer may be asked to pay return shipping to receive the product back, and inspection or repair charges if the customer chooses to proceed with a paid repair.

## Out-of-Warranty Repairs
After the warranty period ends or for excluded damage, paid repair may be available. A quotation is shared before any work begins. Repair is not guaranteed for products that are discontinued or where parts are unavailable.

## Manufacturer Warranty
Some products carry an additional manufacturer warranty. Where applicable, SupportLite Store can guide customers on the nearest manufacturer service center. The terms of the manufacturer's warranty are set by the manufacturer.

## Warranty Transfer
The warranty belongs to the original purchaser and is not transferable unless the product is gifted and the order ID and invoice are provided.

## Limitation
Warranty coverage is limited to the remedies in this policy. SupportLite Store is not liable for indirect losses, data loss, or loss of use, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This policy does not affect statutory consumer rights under Indian law.
