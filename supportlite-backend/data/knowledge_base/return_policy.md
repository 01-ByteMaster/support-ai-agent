
# Return Policy — DEMONSTRATION DATA

Document ID: return_policy
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-01-01
Applies to: All physical products purchased from SupportLite Store
Related documents: refund_policy, warranty_policy, support_procedures

## Return Policy Summary
Customers may return eligible products within 7 calendar days of delivery. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Returns must be authorized by SupportLite Store before the item is sent back.

## Return Window
The return window is 7 calendar days from the delivery date shown in order tracking. A return request must be created within this period. Items sent back without a return authorization (RMA) number may be refused.

## Return Conditions
A product can be returned when:
- It is unused, unwashed, and in resalable condition.
- All original packaging, tags, seals, manuals, and accessories are present.
- Free gifts or bundled items that came with the order are returned together with it.
- The invoice or order ID is available.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned
The following items cannot be returned under the standard return policy:
- Final-sale items.
- Gift cards and digital products.
- Personalized or custom-made products, unless defective or incorrect.
- Hygiene-sensitive products with a broken seal.
- Items damaged by misuse, accident, or normal wear.
- Items without original packaging or with missing parts.

## How to Return an Item
1. Open My Orders and select "Return Item", or contact support.
2. Choose the item and the reason for the return.
3. Receive an RMA number by email.
4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and attach the RMA label or write the RMA number on the parcel.
5. Hand over the parcel to the courier at pickup, or drop it at the courier location given in the instructions.
6. Keep the courier receipt or tracking number until the refund is complete.

## Return Pickup and Return Shipping
- Free reverse pickup is available for most pin codes. If pickup is not available for the customer's address, the customer will be asked to ship the item through a courier of their choice.
- Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or wrong items.
- For returns due to change of mind or wrong size ordered, a return shipping fee of ₹80 may be deducted from the refund.
- If pickup fails twice because the customer is unavailable, the return request may be cancelled.

## Return Reasons and Handling
| Return reason | Return shipping | Outcome options |
|---|---|---|
| Damaged in transit | Paid by SupportLite Store | Refund or replacement |
| Defective on arrival | Paid by SupportLite Store | Refund or replacement |
| Wrong item received | Paid by SupportLite Store | Refund or replacement |
| Size / fit issue | May be deducted (₹80) | Exchange or refund |
| Changed mind | May be deducted (₹80) | Refund |
| Not as described | Paid by SupportLite Store if confirmed | Refund or replacement |

## Exchanges
Exchanges for size, colour, or variant are available within 7 calendar days of delivery, subject to stock. If the desired variant is unavailable, the customer may choose a refund instead. Price differences for exchanges must be paid before the replacement is shipped, or the difference is refunded if the new item costs less.

## Inspection After Return
Returned items are inspected within 2 business days of arrival at the warehouse. The inspection checks condition, completeness, and signs of use. Results are communicated by email and SMS. Approved returns proceed to refund or exchange under the refund_policy.

## Rejected Returns
A return can be rejected when the item is used, damaged by the customer, missing parts, or different from the item originally sent. The customer is notified with the reason. The item may be shipped back at the customer's cost, or held for 15 days awaiting customer instructions before disposal.

## Items Lost During Return
Customers are advised to keep proof of shipment. If a return parcel is lost in transit under a SupportLite-arranged pickup, SupportLite Store will investigate with the courier and will not penalize the customer. For self-shipped returns, the customer is responsible for the parcel until it is received at the warehouse.
