# Cancellation Policy — DEMONSTRATION DATA

Document ID: cancellation_policy

Effective: 2026-01-01
Applies to: All orders placed on SupportLite Store
Related documents: refund_policy, order_modification_policy, shipping_policy, support_procedures

## Cancellation Policy Summary
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched. After dispatch, an order cannot be cancelled, but the customer may refuse delivery or request a return after receiving it, subject to the return policy.

## Cancellation Before Dispatch
An order can be cancelled when its status is Placed, Confirmed, or Packed. These statuses mean the order has not yet been handed to the courier. Cancellation is free of charge and the full amount paid is refunded.

## Cancellation After Dispatch
Once the order status is Shipped, Out for Delivery, or Delivered, the order cannot be cancelled. The customer has these options:
- Refuse the parcel at delivery. The item is returned to the warehouse and a refund is processed after it arrives (typically 5 to 10 business days for the item to return).
- Accept the delivery and submit a return request within 7 calendar days, following the return_policy.
Shipping charges may be non-refundable if the customer refuses delivery without a valid reason.

## How to Cancel an Order
1. Open My Orders and select the order, then choose "Cancel Order"; or contact support with the order ID.
2. Select the reason for cancellation.
3. Receive a cancellation confirmation by email and SMS.
If the "Cancel Order" button is not shown, the order has probably been dispatched.

## Order Status Reference
| Order status | Can it be cancelled? |
|---|---|
| Placed | Yes |
| Confirmed | Yes |
| Packed | Yes, subject to the packing team stopping the shipment; confirmed by email |
| Shipped | No |
| Out for Delivery | No |
| Delivered | No (return policy applies) |
| Cancelled | Already cancelled |

## Personalized and Made-to-Order Items
Personalized, custom-made, and made-to-order items can be cancelled only within 2 hours of placing the order. After this period, production starts and cancellation is not possible.

## Pre-Order Items
Pre-orders can be cancelled until the item is dispatched. Full refunds are issued for cancelled pre-orders.

## Partial Cancellation
If an order contains multiple items, individual items can be cancelled before dispatch. If cancelling an item makes the order ineligible for a free-shipping or promotional offer, the discount or shipping fee is recalculated and the refund is adjusted.

## Cancellations by SupportLite Store
SupportLite Store may cancel an order in these situations:
- The item is out of stock or discontinued.
- Payment could not be verified or was flagged as suspicious.
- The delivery address is outside the serviceable area.
- A pricing or listing error is found.
The customer is notified and receives a full refund for any amount paid.

## Refunds After Cancellation
- Prepaid orders: the refund is initiated to the original payment method within 3 business days of cancellation. Bank or provider settlement time varies and no fixed date is promised.
- Cash on Delivery orders: no payment is collected, so no refund is needed.
- Failed payments: if money was deducted but the order was not created, it is automatically reversed by the payment provider, usually within 5 to 7 business days.

## Cancellation After Failed Delivery
If a delivery fails because the address is wrong, the customer is unreachable, or the parcel is refused, the order may be cancelled automatically after the courier's attempts are exhausted (usually 3 attempts). Refunds are processed after the parcel is received back at the warehouse. Shipping charges may be deducted.

## Subscription or Recurring Orders
Where recurring orders are offered, they can be cancelled before the next billing date from the Subscriptions page. Cancelling after a billing date does not cancel the current order; the cancellation rules above then apply to that order.
