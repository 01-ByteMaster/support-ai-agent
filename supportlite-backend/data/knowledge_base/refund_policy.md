# Refund Policy

**Document Type:** Customer Support Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Policy Status:** Demonstration Policy  

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the eligibility requirements, processing procedures, and exception-handling guidelines for customer refund requests.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

Customers may submit refund requests subject to the following eligibility conditions:

- Refund requests must be submitted within **7 calendar days from the date of delivery**.
- Products must be eligible for a refund and remain **unused and in their original packaging**.
- **Final-sale items are not eligible for refunds**.

Meeting these conditions does not automatically guarantee refund approval. Refund requests remain subject to eligibility review.

## 3. Refund Processing

Once a refund request has been reviewed and approved:

- The refund will be initiated to the **original payment method** used for the purchase.
- The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment provider and the customer's bank.
- No fixed refund settlement date is guaranteed under this policy.

Customers should be advised that refund initiation and the final credit appearing in their account are separate processes.

## 4. Damaged or Incorrect Deliveries

Customers who receive damaged or incorrect products should contact Customer Support and provide the following information:

- Relevant order details.
- A description of the damage or discrepancy.
- Photographs showing the damaged product or incorrect delivery, where applicable.

Support will review these cases individually and determine the appropriate resolution based on the circumstances.

## 5. Policy Limitations

- Refund eligibility is subject to the conditions specified in this policy.
- Final-sale items are excluded from standard refund eligibility.
- Damaged or incorrect deliveries require individual support review.
- Refund processing timelines depend on external payment providers and banks.

## 6. Important Notes

This document is demonstration data created for the SupportLite AI customer-support system. It is not an official commercial refund policy and should be replaced or approved by the relevant business before production deployment.

**End of Policy**
